📰 News Summary:
The Indian market saw a strong upward trend today, with the Nifty 50 closing at 23689.6, up 1.18%. This rally was significantly boosted by the banking sector, with HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank showing robust performance. The metals sector also contributed positively, with the Nifty Metal index gaining 2.04%.

📋 Market Analysis:
Today's market surge was propelled by domestic strength in banking and commodities. The movement suggests investors were rotating into cyclical sectors while global cues around precious metals and US tech added broader context.

💡 Impact Analysis:
For retail investors, today's move highlights the importance of sector diversification and understanding market drivers beyond headlines. While the Nifty advanced broadly, individual stock performance varied significantly.

🎓 Today's Lesson:
Sector rotation happens when investors shift money from one sector to another based on changing expectations. Today’s metal and banking strength is one example of how market leadership can change across sectors.
